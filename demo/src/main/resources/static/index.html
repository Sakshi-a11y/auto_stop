Rules & Regulations

Overloading damages roads, increases accident risk, and violates transport laws. Registered vehicles are tracked through automated weighbridge monitoring and this portal.

Permissible Load

Every vehicle must carry weight within the limit approved for its type and registration.

Weighbridge Check

Vehicles inspected at checkpoints; any excess load is recorded as a violation.

Strike System

Each overload event adds one strike to the vehicle record on this portal.

Blocking Policy

After 3 strikes, vehicle status becomes blocked until the prescribed fine is paid.

Fine & Clearance

Owners pay the fine online to reset strikes and restore active status.

Owner Duty

Registered owners must ensure load compliance before every trip.
